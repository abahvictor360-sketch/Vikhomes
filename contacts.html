Common Questions

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

How do I book a viewing?

Open any home, fill in the “Book a viewing” form with your preferred date, and an agent will contact you to confirm a time.

Are listings verified?

Yes. Every home is checked by our team before it goes live, including ownership and the details shown on the listing.

How do I list my home on Vik Homes?

Send us a message through the contact form and choose “Selling or listing a home”. An agent will arrange a visit and help you prepare the listing.
