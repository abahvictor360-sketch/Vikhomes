## Before you post: quick checklist

- [ ] **Say it's a concept project.** The site footer already says the listings are sample content. Call it a "concept" or "personal project" in posts too, so nobody mistakes it for a real agency.
- [ ] **Credit the design inspiration.** The homepage layout follows a design mockup that isn't yours. If you know who made it, add "Design inspired by [designer]" to Behance/Dribbble posts. Clients respect honesty, and it avoids disputes.
- [ ] **Check the photos.** The house images came from the web. For a portfolio that's usually fine, but for client work use licensed photos (e.g. Unsplash, Pexels) or the client's own.
- [ ] **Add your contact details.** Put your email, WhatsApp or portfolio link in your profile bio so people can reach you.
- [ ] **Pin the post** to the top of your LinkedIn/Instagram/X profile.
